---
name: AI Overview / AI Mode claims require independent verification
description: Google AI Mode / AI Overview answers can present confident, specific-sounding but fabricated details (exact taglines, "iconic" campaigns, sourced-feeling backstory) with no real grounding — verify before using in evidence, copy, or filings.
---

When a user shares an AI Mode/AI Overview answer as a lead (e.g. "AI told me X company ran Y famous campaign"), treat it as a hypothesis, not a fact — even when phrased confidently and specifically.

**Why:** In one case, an AI Mode answer invented an "iconic, long-running TV commercial campaign" for a real company, when in reality the AI had just pattern-matched the target's *current* website tagline onto a fabricated historical narrative. Meanwhile, a separate factual claim in the same AI answer (a business-to-business relationship between two named companies) turned out to be genuinely grounded and led to real, independently verifiable evidence in official state records.

**How to apply:** Split every AI-sourced claim into checkable atoms (company names, entity relationships, dates) and verify each via primary sources — for Florida business entities, `search.sunbiz.org` gives authoritative filing history, officers, and name-change chains and is far stronger evidence than any AI summary or even a Wayback Machine capture. Use `webSearch`/`webFetch` to pull the primary record directly rather than quoting the AI's paraphrase. Only carry forward the atoms that check out; explicitly discard and flag the ones that don't.
