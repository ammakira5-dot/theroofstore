## Context
The Roof Store's core pitch is coating instead of replacing — at roughly half the cost. This positions them perfectly in the "roof replacement alternative" keyword tier on Google, which has lower competition and CPC than pure replacement keywords.

## Key Findings (researched 2026-07-07)

**Industry CPC benchmarks (Florida roofing):**
- Tier 1 (replacement keywords): $40–$95/click — dominated by big contractors, not our fight
- Tier 2 (alternative/restoration keywords): $20–$55/click — our sweet spot
- Tier 3 (long-tail): $10–$30/click — good for SEO-first, ads-second

**Best-fit keywords for theroofstore.net:**

| Keyword | Est. CPC (FL) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `roof restoration vs replacement Florida` | $20–$45 | Decision-stage, perfect match |
| `roof coating instead of replacement` | $20–$50 | Exact intent |
| `flat roof coating Florida` | $20–$45 | Direct product match |
| `tile roof restoration Florida` | $25–$55 | Direct service match |
| `roof repair vs full replacement cost` | $12–$25 | Long-tail, lower cost |
| `can I coat my roof instead of replacing it` | $10–$22 | Conversational, high intent |
| `roof coating cost per square foot Florida` | $10–$22 | Budget-stage buyer |
| `roof replacement alternative Florida` | $20–$45 | Core long-tail target |
| `tile roof restoration instead of replacement` | $20–$40 | Fits Windproof + Roof Store |
| `how to avoid roof replacement Florida` | $8–$18 | Informational, high buyer intent |

**The homeowner question phrase (user liked this framing):**
> "homeowner actively considering whether to replace vs. coat their roof"
→ Maps to: `"can I coat my roof instead of replacing it"` and `"roof coating instead of replacement"`

## Recommended Next Steps (pending user review)

1. **Build a dedicated landing page** — `/roof-replacement-alternative-florida` — targeting these keywords organically (SEO first, ads second). This page would own the decision-stage buyer before any ad spend.
2. **Add phrase naturally to existing pages** — especially `/roof-painting-vs-coating` as an H2 heading. Not in homepage `<title>` tag (too niche for homepage primary signal).
3. **Consider Google LSAs** (Local Services Ads) — pay per lead not per click, appear above all other ads with "Google Guaranteed" badge.

## Why Not Tier 1 Keywords?
`roof replacement near me` ($40–$95/click) is dominated by replacement contractors. Bidding there puts The Roof Store against competitors doing something entirely different. The alternative/restoration angle at $10–$45/click is the strategic gap.
