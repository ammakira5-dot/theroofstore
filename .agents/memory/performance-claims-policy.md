---
name: Performance claims policy
description: Owner-confirmed rule for publishing product performance numbers (wind/uplift, etc.)
---

Publish only (a) test-verified figures (e.g. 135+ MPH under the Dade County Certified TAS-106 Uplift Test — NOTE: the TEST is certified, the product is NOT "certified" and holds NO NOA; never claim product certification) and (b) raw measurements stated factually (e.g. "100 lbf pull, no movement — nearly 3× the pass standard"). Never publish extrapolated numbers (e.g. converting pull force to an implied wind speed) even when mathematically defensible.

**Why:** Wind force scales with velocity squared, so linear extrapolations overstate wildly; and one attackable number lets competitors/opposing counsel discredit the whole page — including the certified claims. Owner explicitly agreed (Aug 3, 2026) after wanting to claim "several hundred MPH."

**How to apply:** When owner supplies impressive test results, anchor to the official standard first, state the raw measurement, explain the mechanism, and let readers extrapolate themselves. Same discipline as the evidence pages (facts, never argument).
