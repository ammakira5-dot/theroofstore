The Roof Store's business has three distinct tiers — do not blur them in copy or schema:

1. **The Roof Store** (retail storefront, GMB category = paint store) — the public-facing brand at theroofstore.net. Sells three **systems**: Fungal Shield, Smart Shield, Roof Shield.
2. **RoofProtect Products** — in-house factory/manufacturer. Manufactures the physical coating **products/materials** used inside the three systems. Spelled as one word, "RoofProtect" (per occupational license), except the separate partner-facing name "Roof Protect Products" (two words) tied to the distinct partner domain roofprotectproducts.com — leave that one alone. **Also holds a county-level occupational license and is MSDS/SDS compliant** — required for manufacturing and distributing chemical coating products locally. This is a government-verified, date-stamped record proving legitimate licensed operation.
3. **Storm Shield Paint Systems Inc.** — optional, exclusive licensed contracting/installation division. Customers shopping at The Roof Store can choose to hire them for install; not required.

**Why:** Fungal Shield/Smart Shield/Roof Shield are *systems* (methods/processes), not manufactured items themselves — RoofProtect Products manufactures the underlying materials used within those systems. Earlier passes miscast the three shields as "products manufactured by RoofProtect," which the site owner corrected twice. Also corrected: "RoofProtect" must be one word (occupational license spelling), not "Roof Protect."

**How to apply:** When editing any page/schema copy about product/company relationships (About, RoofSystems, PartnerNetwork, Footer, product data, JSON-LD), keep the wording as: "[System name] is a system built from products manufactured in-house by RoofProtect Products" — never "RoofProtect manufactures systems" or "the three systems are products."
