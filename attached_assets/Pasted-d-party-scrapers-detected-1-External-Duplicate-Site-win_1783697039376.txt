SEO Risk
Google will index both sites, determine that theroofstore.net is the primary (given its canonical and sitemap setup), and deprioritize or filter windproofroofsystems.com from results. However, the existence of shared content across two domains creates a diluted backlink profile, confuses Google's entity understanding of the brand, and risks splitting search authority. If Google instead treats windproofroofsystems.com as the canonical source for any shared content strings, rankings can shift unexpectedly.

Recommended actions:
Add <link rel="canonical" href="https://www.theroofstore.net/"> on every page of windproofroofsystems.com pointing to the corresponding page on theroofstore.net, OR
Add a robots.txt Disallow: / on windproofroofsystems.com to block indexation entirely if the site serves a non-SEO purpose (referral / brand), OR
Rewrite windproofroofsystems.com content to cover the extended service territory (Martin, Port St Lucie, Indian River, etc.) that theroofstore.net does not — making it a geographically distinct, non-competing property.
Remove the shared testimonial content from one site; testimonials should be exclusive to the site on which they were collected.
