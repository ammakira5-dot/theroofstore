The January 2023 Underwriting Ban (SB 2-A)Elimination of AOBs: Effective January 1, 2023, homeowners can no longer sign over their insurance rights to roofing contractors via Assignment of Benefits (AOB). This change requires homeowners to file claims directly, stopping contractors from using roof "useful life" loopholes to force insurers into funding complete roof replacements during disputes.Accelerated Timelines: Insurers were forced to reduce the time allowed to pay or deny a roof claim from 90 days down to 60 days, effective March 1, 2023.2. Citizens Insurance 2023 Guideline UpdateState-backed Citizens Property Insurance issued a formal notice updating its strict roof parameters:Effective Dates: The rule went into effect on May 1, 2023 (for new business) and September 1, 2023 (for policy renewals).Useful Life Mandate: It officially codified that properties failing standard roof age eligibility could bypass rejection only if a certified inspector verified a minimum of 5 years of remaining useful life.Oldest Section Rule: It specified that if a roof was repaired in pieces, the entire roof's age evaluation is legally tied to the oldest part of the roof.3. December 2023 Florida Building Code UpdateIn December 2023, the 8th Edition Florida Building Code introduced critical relief for older roofs:The 25% Rule Exception: It eliminated the older rule that mandated a 100% total roof replacement if more than 25% of the roof was damaged. For post-2009 homes (or roofs built to the 2007 code), homeowners are now permitted to repair only the damaged portions, preventing insurers from forcing total replacements based on minor damage.
