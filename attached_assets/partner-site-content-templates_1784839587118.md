**H1:** How to Evaluate a Roof Coating Contractor — A Technical Buyer's Guide

**Unique intro:**
> Best Roof Paint and Waterproofing Service exists for one reason: most homeowners can't tell a roofing-grade coating from a repackaged paint product until it fails. We built this site around the questions a genuinely informed buyer should ask before hiring anyone — dry film thickness, ponding water ratings, surface prep requirements, and manufacturer warranty terms — because those specifics are what actually determine whether a coating lasts 3 years or 15.

**Differentiator section — "What to Actually Check Before You Buy":**
- Dry film thickness (roofing-grade coatings run 20+ mils; decorative paint runs 2–5 mils)
- Ponding water rating — critical for flat roofs, and something most standard elastomeric coatings are not rated for
- Written manufacturer warranty terms, not just a contractor's verbal guarantee
- Surface preparation requirements — a coating is only as good as the cleaning and repair work done before it's applied

**Local service section:**
> Serving Martin County, St. Lucie, Palm Beach, Broward, and Miami-Dade — including Port St. Lucie, Fort Pierce, and the greater Treasure Coast region.
