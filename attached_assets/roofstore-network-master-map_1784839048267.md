- **#5 (Hollywood, Roof Shield Coating System)** — no access, and the profile itself has a broken website URL configured, meaning anyone searching for it hits a dead link right now

If anyone else ever gained admin control of that specific profile, they could edit business hours, respond to reviews as "you," or redirect the listed website — under your own legal company name.

**What to do:** Google has a standard "Request Access" flow for business profiles you don't currently administer (Business Profile Manager → search the business name/address → "Request access" → verification by phone/postcard/email to the current owner on file, or an ownership dispute process if the current owner is unresponsive). This can take anywhere from a few days to a few weeks depending on whether the current admin responds. Given the active trademark dispute you're already documenting, recovering control of a Google listing that carries your exact legal entity name should be treated as a priority action item, not a someday task.
