2. New Issues Found — Consistency & Compliance

🔴 Insurance/ARLC Messaging Is Now Inconsistent Across Pages

The homepage and the new /roof-life-certification page have been rewritten with more legally precise language:

"No contractor certifies your roof's useful life. No product does. The right is yours." — /roof-life-certification

"It is that licensed inspector's report — not any product or contractor — that can support a determination of additional useful life." — Homepage FAQ

This is a more accurate and defensible position: only a licensed inspector's Useful Life Exam report is what Florida law requires insurers to recognize — not a contractor-issued document. However, two other pages still make the stronger, uncorrected claim:

"The Roof Store can issue Additional Roof Life Certifications for roofs that have received a qualifying coating system." — /roof-coating-florida

"✅ The Roof Store Handles ARLC Documentation — As both the product manufacturer and the licensed contractor, The Roof Store can issue Additional Roof Life Certifications directly for all Roof Shield (RP3) installations." — /tas-106-certified-roof-coating

Why this matters: Beyond SEO, this is a customer-facing accuracy issue. If the corrected legal position (inspector issues the report, not the contractor) is the accurate one, the pillar and TAS-106 pages are currently making a stronger claim than the company can legally stand behind. If the older claim is actually correct and the homeower/roof-life-certification pages are the ones that changed, then those two pages have been intentionally softened and the pillar/TAS-106 pages are now the outdated ones. Either way, all four pages need to say the same thing.

Recommended fix: Align all pages to the /roof-life-certification page's framing: The Roof Store prepares the roof (clean, repair, coat) and can assist with documentation/paperwork, but the licensed inspector's Useful Life Exam report — not a contractor certification — is what the insurer is legally required to recognize. Update /roof-coating-florida and /tas-106-certified-roof-coating to match this framing.

⚠️ TAS-106 Certification Year — 2006 vs. 2023 Discrepancy

Audit #1 and #2 (and the pillar page still) state the TAS-106 certification was "issued 2023" / "most recently in 2023." The new /tas-106-certified-roof-coating page states it was "originally issued 2006" and displays "2006 · Certification issued" as a stat card. These may both be true (originally issued 2006, renewed/updated 2023) but the pillar page's "certified most recently in 2023" claim and the TAS-106 page's "issued 2006" framing read as contradictory if a reader compares them.

Fix: State both dates together wherever the certification date appears: "Originally certified in 2006, with current Notice of Acceptance renewal in 2023." This is a stronger, more credible claim than either date alone and resolves the apparent conflict.

⚠️ Embedded YouTube Videos Showing Error State During Crawl

The TAS-106 page crawl captured two instances of H1: An error occurred. in the heading structure — this appears to originate from embedded YouTube video iframes ("Uplift Test" and the Hurricane Wilma testimonial video) failing to render during the crawl. This may be a crawler-side rendering artifact rather than a real user-facing bug, but it's worth a manual check.

Action: Load the live TAS-106 page in a browser and confirm both YouTube embeds play correctly. If they do, this is a crawler artifact and not a real issue. If either video shows an actual error to real visitors, that's a trust signal problem on your best GEO page and should be fixed immediately — broken video embeds undercut the "here's the proof" narrative the page is built around.

⚠️ Parallel URL Structure: /service-areas/[county] vs. /roof-services/[county]

The sitemap now lists both /service-areas/broward-county and /roof-services/broward-county (and the same pattern for Miami-Dade and Palm Beach). If these serve duplicate or near-duplicate content under two URLs, it creates internal cannibalization — Google may split ranking signals between them instead of consolidating on one.

Action: Confirm whether these are duplicate content or genuinely distinct pages (e.g., one for "service areas" overview, one for "services by county" listing). If duplicate, canonicalize to one URL and 301 the other, then remove the redirected URL from the sitemap.

⚠️ Main Navigation — "Roof Coating Guide" Link Visibility Unclear

Audit #2 confirmed "Roof Coating Guide" was added to the main nav. In this audit's crawl, the visible nav on the TAS-106 and Fort Lauderdale pages reads: Products · About Us · Services · Service Areas · Reviews · Contact Us · More — without a visible "Roof Coating Guide" or "Roof Systems" item. This may be a crawler timing/hydration artifact rather than a real regression, since the homepage crawl in this same session still showed the fuller nav.

Action: Manually verify the nav on a live page load (not just homepage) to confirm "Roof Coating Guide" is consistently visible site-wide, including on interior pages, not just the homepage.
