1. Confirmed Fixes Since Audit #3

✅ Pricing Table Sync — Fully Resolved

Audit #3 found the /roof-coating-florida pillar page showing outdated pricing ($3.10–$3.90 for RP3 barrel tile) while the live /pricing page had moved to higher rates ($4.00–$4.75). Both pages now show identical numbers across every system, roof type, and pitch combination. This was a P1 item — fully closed.

✅ Cost-Savings Percentage — Standardized

Audit #3 found three different savings percentages floating across the site (40–60% / 60–70% / 70–80%). The main content on both /roof-coating-florida and /pricing now consistently states 60–70%. This is the single number that should now propagate to any remaining pages still showing 40–60% (homepage FAQ, city pages) — worth a final sweep.

✅ TAS-106 Certification Date — Fixed Exactly as Recommended

Audit #3 flagged an apparent contradiction: the pillar page said "certified most recently in 2023" while the dedicated TAS-106 page said "originally issued 2006." The pillar page now reads: "The Roof Shield system was originally certified in 2006 — the only liquid coating to ever achieve this standard — with current NOA renewal in 2023." This is precisely the dual-date framing recommended — resolved cleanly.

3. Still Open From Prior Audits

🔴 ARLC / Insurance Messaging — Unchanged

The pillar page still states: "The Roof Store can issue Additional Roof Life Certifications for roofs that have received a qualifying coating system." This is the same stronger claim flagged in Audit #3 as inconsistent with the more legally precise framing already used on /roof-life-certification ("no contractor certifies your roof's useful life... it is the inspector's report"). No change detected on either the pillar page or the TAS-106 page.

🔴 Duplicate Meta Tag Bug — Not Re-Verified This Round

This P1 item from Audit #1 was still open as of Audit #3. It wasn't specifically re-checked in this round's crawl — recommend a direct verification pass since it's the oldest unresolved item across all four audits.
