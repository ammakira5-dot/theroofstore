2. Pillar Page — Critical Redirect Issue

🔴 /roof-coating-florida Redirects to /roof-services — URL Conflict

The crawl of https://theroofstore.net/roof-coating-florida returns the /roof-services page content — the canonical URL in the parsed data is https://www.theroofstore.net/roof-services, not /roof-coating-florida. This means one of two things is happening:

  • The URL /roof-coating-florida is redirecting (301 or 302) to /roof-services, OR
  • The pillar page content was published under /roof-services instead of the intended /roof-coating-florida slug
Impact: The keyword target "roof coating Florida" is being served under the URL /roof-services — a weaker slug with no keyword signal. The nav item "Roof Coating Guide" points to this page, so the navigation is correct, but the URL itself is not optimal.

Fix option A (preferred): Publish the pillar content at /roof-coating-florida as a distinct page separate from /roof-services. The services page and the pillar guide serve different intents and both can exist.

Fix option B: If you intentionally merged the content into /roof-services, redirect /roof-coating-florida to /roof-services with a 301 and update all internal links and the sitemap entry to point to /roof-services as the canonical URL.

4. Still Open — Remaining Issues

🔴 Duplicate Meta Tag Bug — Still Present on All Pages

Every page on the site still renders all meta tags twice in the <head>. This was P1 in Audit #1 and remains unfixed. Confirmed present on homepage, pricing, FAQ, blog, and roof-services pages.

Fix: Find the component in your framework that injects meta tags and ensure it fires only once per render. Common cause: a layout component and a page component both calling the same meta injection function. This is the single most impactful remaining technical fix — it affects every page on the site.

⚠️ Review Count — 9 → 10 (Target: 50+)

Only one review was added between audits. The aggregateRating in schema now shows reviewCount: 10. The 90-day sprint target of 50+ reviews is critical for unlocking Rich Result star displays and Local Pack competitiveness. At the current pace of ~1 review per 3 days, reaching 50 would take approximately 4 months. A systematic post-job review request process needs to be active.

Action: Set up an automated SMS/email trigger 48 hours after job completion. One message, one direct Google Review link, no friction. This single workflow change should produce 5–10× the current review velocity.

⚠️ Pillar Page URL — Redirect Conflict Needs Resolution

As documented in Section 2, /roof-coating-florida currently serves the /roof-services content. The pillar intent and the services page intent are different enough that they warrant separate pages. Resolved in the next sprint.

⚠️ Individual Blog Post Pages — BlogPosting Schema Unverified

The three new July 2026 blog posts need individual-page verification. The blog index correctly includes BlogPosting schema for all 7 posts, but each individual post URL (/blog/[slug]/) should also have its own BlogPosting schema with author, datePublished, dateModified, headline, and image. Without it, individual posts are not eligible for Article Rich Results.

Check: Visit any individual blog post URL, view source, and confirm "@type":"BlogPosting" is present in a JSON-LD script block on that page specifically.

⚠️ City Pages — Differentiation Not Yet Started

100+ city service area pages remain on the same template pattern. This was a P1 item in Audit #1. The content architecture and keyword work from this session provide everything needed to start differentiation — long-tail keywords per county, differentiation formula, and priority city list are all documented in the brand reference file.

⚠️ Pricing Page Word Count Still Low — 646 Words

Despite the H1, title, schema, and meta improvements, the pricing page body content is still only 646 words. The target was 1,000–1,200 words with pricing-specific FAQs, a cost comparison section, and factors affecting price. The structural fixes are done; the content expansion remains.

⚠️ TAS-106 Dedicated Page — Not Yet Created

The most powerful GEO citation asset identified in Audit #1 — a standalone /tas-106-certified-roof-coating page — does not yet exist. This remains the single highest-value content creation opportunity on the site: a category monopoly no competitor can ever replicate.
