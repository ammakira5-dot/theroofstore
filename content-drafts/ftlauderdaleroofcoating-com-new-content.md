## About

Ft. Lauderdale Roof Coating provides professional roof coating, waterproofing, and roof painting services to homeowners and property managers throughout South Florida. As an authorized applicator of The Roof Store's coating line — FungalShield, SmartShield, and RoofShield — we bring manufacturer-trained application standards to every job, from a single-family home to multi-unit properties.

We specialize in stopping leaks before they start: sealing tile and flat roofs against South Florida's heavy rain and heat with flexible, long-lasting membrane systems, while keeping surfaces looking freshly finished.
